# Style menu: formats, skins and effects

Every video is three choices:
- a **format** (the shape of the video)
- a **skin** (the look)
- **effects** (the moves)

Mix them freely. Examples of every one are on the showcase page (`python3 scripts/serve.py`, then http://localhost:4870/showcase/index.html). How to make each one: `FORMATS.md`. Add your own styles here as you create them (see "Grow your own styles" in START-HERE.md).

## Formats

- **Clean** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: Any · You bring: A recording of you talking to camera · Good for: Quick updates, podcasts, talking-head posts. **Best first video.**
- **Explainer** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 5–20 min · You bring: A recording of you talking to camera · Good for: YouTube long-form, teaching, opinion pieces
- **Case Study** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 2–4 min · You bring: A customer interview, their real numbers and logo (with their OK) · Good for: Sales assets, testimonials, proof for your website
- **Paper Cards** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 5–20 min · You bring: A recording of you talking to camera · Good for: YouTube with data and punchlines: before/after numbers
- **Napkin Sketch** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 2–10 min · You bring: Your voiceover and its script · Good for: Concept videos, "how X works"
- **Marker Paper** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 1–3 min · You bring: A plain-speak script, then your voiceover · Good for: "[Topic] for busy owners" explainers, one simple idea per line
- **Animated Sketch** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 1–10 min · You bring: Your voiceover, its script and a music track · Good for: Story-led concept videos with energy
- **Screen Demo** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 1–15 min · You bring: A screen recording (plus an optional webcam recording) · Good for: Tutorials, onboarding, product walkthroughs
- **Hype Reel** · Shape: 9:16 · Length: 30–90 s · You bring: A clip of you talking (any orientation) · Good for: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
- **Why It Worked** · Shape: 9:16 · Length: 30–60 s · You bring: A ~100-word script, a hook idea, a freebie and your voiceover · Good for: Explaining why a result happened, with a lead magnet
- **Sizzle Reel** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 20–40 s · You bring: A short brief (product, 3–5 messages, call to action) and a music track · Good for: Launches, features, ads, website heroes
- **Guided Tour** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: 30 s–3 min · You bring: Screenshots (or a website address) and the steps · Good for: Onboarding, how-tos, faceless walkthroughs
- **AI Call Replay** · Shape: 16:9 · Length: Length of the call · You bring: A call recording (with everyone's OK) and its transcript · Good for: Showing an AI receptionist or voice agent at work

## Skins

Use one as a starting point, then set your own colours, fonts and caption style in `brand.json`. Caption styles in the toolkit: `pill`, `karaoke`, `box`, `upper`.

- **Classic:** two colours, one bold font, no shadows, a tilted sticker on the key phrase. Best for thumbnails and hooks.
- **Modern:** charcoal and off-white, a serif for big words, a clean sans for everything else, glass cards. The all-rounder.
- **Editorial:** a magazine feel, with serif captions on a solid plate and italic punch lines. Best for premium-feeling talking-head reels.
- **Glass:** everything frosted glass, neutral greys, the spoken word highlighted, huge words behind your head. Best for tool demos and comparisons.
- **Layered:** you move between full screen, half and a ringed bubble, with karaoke captions and typed headlines. Best for longer tutorials.
- **Product:** ultra-clean, soft slate and white, words that blur in one by one. Best for feature launches.
- **Tech:** dark, chrome headlines, mono labels, thin outline cards on a grid. Best for AI and automation builds.

## Effects

Effects are the building blocks inside any format. Some favourites:
- word-by-word captions
- punch-in camera angles
- words behind your head
- split screens
- tool logo intros
- counters that tick up
- checklists that build
- chat threads
- a button with a cursor click
- an end card

The starter showcase has two labelled reels with over 100 effects you can jump through. Pick the ones you like and name them in the video's notes.txt.
